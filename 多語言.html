1、The Antichrist will come at a time when people's moral standards are at an all time low. People will think they are following Jesus, but they will really be following the Antichrist. Only the very elect will be safe during this time.
反基督将在人们道德标准降至历史最低点的时候到来。人们会以为自己是在跟随耶稣，但他们实际上是在跟随反基督。只有极少数的选民在这段时期会是安全的。
